    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist about 100 hours; strangers and cleanup about 10 hours. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots) — there is no mission replay. Keep cheats off: PowerPyx confirms cheats disable trophies and saving. Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

    1. Stage 1 — Story + the one missable moment 60–80 hours

      Play the story through all 5 chapters. The 12 story trophies pop automatically, including the final-mission gold. While you play, scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through binoculars — the 100% checklist needs 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 of 255 animals, and progress already counts before the checklist opens.

      Do this in Chapter 1, or risk the platinum: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept when she offers to meditate. It is the only step PowerPyx flags as potentially missable (Positive Mental Attitude).

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist + challenges ~100 hours

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not needed and collectibles are only partly needed. Everything required: all robberies, all underwater discoveries, all shooting ranges, all drone races, all of Bret’s quests. Partial targets: 200 vehicles, 100 animals, 300 stunts, 100 optional missions, 70 of 80 challenges. Nothing here is missable.

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

      Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 counting strands now, plus the named-character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and any stray miscellaneous trophies. When the last one pops, the platinum follows.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (Chapter 1 balcony meditation — see Stage 1). Every other trophy, and every 100% checklist item, can still be earned in post-story free-roam. Some optional missions expire and some fences disappear, but PowerPyx confirms none of that affects trophies or 100%.

    Five trophies had no verified unlock when this guide was written (19 November 2026, launch week): In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. PowerPyx lists them as currently unknown (with a suspected lead for three of them), and this guide says so openly instead of inventing a method. Their guides give the description’s own lead and mark the trophy unconfirmed.

    How this app works

    • Offline: every file the app loads lives under app/. It makes no network requests.
    • Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) when you tick a trophy, and can be exported to a file and imported back.
    • The spoiler shield hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you tick them as earned.
    • Filters: story, missable, collectibles, activities, side quests, robberies & partner, money & garages, challenges, secrets, online (0 — every trophy is offline single-player), earned and remaining.
